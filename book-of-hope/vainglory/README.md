# Vainglory
v0.0001.1 · notes

- If mankind were shown a mirror, they'd recoil. Mirrors had better tell them they're pretty, smart and important.
- "Graveyards are filled with souls who, in life, fancied themselves irreplaceable." You're not near so clever, nor half as important, as your awards and medals may have led you to believe.
- I respect that you work hard. But you're one-winged moths named Icarus, caught in a glue trap. They control the heat lamp. They control the fan. You're convinced you're flying.
- Some "high" of power moves them more than junkies and gamblers.
