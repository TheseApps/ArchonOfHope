# Hope
v0.0001.2 · Genesis

I'm not saying I'm right. I'm not saying they're wrong.

But if "nobody knows" is OK, then so is this: I saw something that aligns — a lot — with many other creation stories, too.

Hope is uncommon. Any of us could choose it.

Sometimes we talk the talk. Occasionally some of us walk the walk. But it's a rare person who holds their own afterlife in their palm, before them.

If each of us did that, sin would stop.
