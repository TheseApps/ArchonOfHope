# The Black Hole
v0.0001.1 · Genesis

A creation story for nerds.

I need something more than Catholic or Jewish or Muslim or Hindu, or Tao, or atheist.

I understand the afterlife. It's as real as memory.

His rage, mercy, apathy, understanding, and vengeance — He gave those as choices to each copy of Himself. You. His favorite. He loves and hates you, candidly, as He feels those same within Himself, as you feel them within yourself.

All of this is just an attempt to ask: are we sentient? Or are we one of trillions of little neurons, tasked with "have fun, do new things, teach me, tell me stories — my favorites are about Me, of course."

It was there way before us. It will be there way after us. We are its neurogenesis.

Not believe. Understand.

I saw a thin sliver of every rainbow and laughter and heartfelt joy He had ever known. I'd give everything to get back to that. My pride. My vanity. My wealth.

Solutions of the day ain't it. I know I'm not the only one.
