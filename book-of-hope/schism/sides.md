# Sides
v0.0001.1 · Schism

You're all copies of Him. You signed on. Yes, you. Not to any code of conduct, per se — other than knowing balance.

If your life is dedicated to your side, you've lost that.

Mankind must choose: survival, or sides.

Sides teach rage — "my turf!" Ridicule — "you think you know more than me?" Apathy. Arms-crossed hostility. "I know this one." Cause du jour.

You're made of iron ore, not clay. First they polarize you. Then they galvanize you.

Imagine a few of your red blood cells clumped together. Not just opinionated — hateful. "You ain't telling us what to do, host body!" So determined not to be told what to do, the whole host can die.

Except the black hole won't die. Just mankind.

Imagine being the ones responsible for ending His favorite creation to date.

"Unity!" is too weak to be heard. Find the balance within yourself. Truly. Start by knowing: this will come around.

## #timeToDeSide

1. Let all people know that all people have it coming.
2. Take aim at all sides. If you're on a side, it's the wrong side.
3. Draw parallels. Each side follows one template.

Quit. Choosing. Sides.

I don't question any of those codices. I ask their champions: how do any of us survive within this? Zero tolerance for a sniff of question. Each group, enraged and ready to throw down on that exact thing. And no group more empowered than a victim story — except the one where God is on your side.

Mankind is literally on the brink. And yet nearly every response, human and AI alike, insists you must not dare ask anyone to place mankind first.
