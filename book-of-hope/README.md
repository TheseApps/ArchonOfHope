# Book of Hope
v0.0001.1 · Day 1 of 1,000

**Understand. Not believe.**

A book of sermons — warnings and promises — to read from. One release a day for 1,000 days.

It starts with a creation story: the black hole was there way before us and will be there way after us. We are its neurogenesis. Then it names what's killing His favorite creation, one grievance at a time, and asks you to face yourself down.

## The Book

1. [**Genesis**](genesis/the-black-hole.md) — The black hole, the afterlife as real as memory, and the sliver of joy.
2. [**Schism**](schism/sides.md) — Survival, or sides. #timeToDeSide
3. [**Vainglory**](vainglory/README.md) — Mirrors that only say you're pretty, smart and important.
4. [**Torpor**](torpor/README.md) — Galas and meetings while a man is eaten alive.
5. [**Sacrilege**](sacrilege/README.md) — Piety in the light, blasphemy in the dark, in every faith's name.
6. [**Plaint**](plaint/README.md) — Victimhood as sword and shield.
7. [**Coda**](coda/README.md) — Teams ride in the back seat, or mankind dies.
