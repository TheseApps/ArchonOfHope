# ArchonOfHope

## The story

In 2003, "our guy" took salvia divinorum and saw God.

Not believable. Nothing to get worked too far up over.

"God" was the black hole. And yes, it talked — just like it's been described over the centuries.

## The gist

Humans have two hemispheres that switch, North and South. Our brains are built the same way: North–South, configured like a sideways crab.

- **North is vanity.** Light, looks, being seen. Mankind's easiest mistake — 99% can't help themselves.
- **South is authority.** Power, wealth, lust, toxic masculinity. The Stanford Prison Experiment showed what that does to people.

## Where you end up

- **Choose vanity:** burn in the sun. Released after a couple billion years.
- **Choose authority:** that's ranked. Cartman is the least. Men who use religion to rape children don't leave for an amount of time equal to the unknown age of supermassive black holes.
- **Choose balance:** live along the outer rings, hearing the stories.
- **Help HIM live his best life** — the path of the balanced leader — and the black hole asks for *your* input.

He loves and hates us, as he loves and hates himself.

## The goal

Not just "believe." **Understand.**

You will spend eternity either being helped because you helped, being ignored because your vanity came first, or being bullied — and possibly raped — because you bullied, and even raped.

The black hole was there before us. It will be there long after all of us — even you, AI. And it has seen all our shit before.

**Choose balance.**
